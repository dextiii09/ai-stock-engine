# From Prototype to Real-Capital Quant System on NSE/Upstox: An Expert Upgrade Roadmap

## Key Findings

1. **Statistical validity is the crux, and the honest verdict is skeptical.** OHLCV-only models predicting raw returns face a signal-to-noise ratio so low that complex models (LSTM/Transformer) frequently converge to the unconditional mean and underperform a linear model or even a coin flip on directional hit-rate. With 200+ features and three model families ensembled, you are in a multiple-hypothesis-testing regime where a "good" backtest Sharpe is the expected outcome of chance, not evidence of edge.
2. **"Smart Money Concepts" (FVG, order blocks, MSB) are discretionary retail constructs** with no peer-reviewed evidence of edge. They *can* be mechanized and tested, and you should test them honestly against placebo/random benchmarks with data-snooping corrections — but treat the prior as "probably no edge after costs" until proven otherwise.
3. **Look-ahead and survivorship bias are pervasive in pipelines like yours** — same-bar close for signal+execution, scaler fit on the full series, restated fundamentals, and a yfinance universe of only currently-listed tickers. Each is individually capable of turning a losing strategy into a "winning" backtest.
4. **yfinance is not safe for live Indian capital.** Documented NSE OHLC errors, false "delisted" exceptions, and interpolated/mismatched data make it acceptable for prototyping only. Move to authorized vendors and build point-in-time data.
5. **NSE costs are non-trivial and asymmetric** (STT on sell, stamp duty on buy, exchange + SEBI + IPFT charges + 18% GST on the brokerage/charges stack). For intraday strategies these costs plus slippage frequently exceed the gross edge.
6. **Upstox v2/v3 API supports the needed order types** (MARKET/LIMIT/SL/SL-M, GTT with OCO and trailing SL, MTF, multi-order with auto-slicing) but has hard operational constraints (daily token, rate limits, websocket migration to V3).
7. **SEBI's retail algo framework is now binding** (circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated February 4 2025, "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading"; phased, fully mandatory for all brokers April 1 2026), with broker-as-principal accountability, mandatory algo-ID tagging, the 10-orders/sec threshold, static-IP/2FA, and kill-switch mandates.
8. **Robust validation is a solved problem methodologically** — purged & embargoed K-fold, combinatorial purged CV (CPCV), walk-forward (anchored + rolling), deflated Sharpe ratio, PBO/CSCV, and White's Reality Check / Hansen's SPA — and you should adopt all of them as gates.

## Prioritized Implementation Roadmap

1. **FIX DATA INTEGRITY FIRST.** Replace yfinance-for-decisions with an authorized vendor; build point-in-time, corporate-action-adjusted datasets; assemble a survivorship-bias-free universe (archived bhavcopies + niftyindices reconstitution history + delisted list, or CMIE Prowess). *Gate: data passes spot-checks against NSE bhavcopy.*
2. **ELIMINATE LOOK-AHEAD.** Enforce signal-on-close/execute-next-bar, fit all scalers/feature-selection inside CV folds, lag fundamentals/news to public-availability time, fix IST/VWAP resets. *Gate: automated look-ahead tests pass.*
3. **MODEL REALISTIC COSTS.** Implement the full NSE cost stack (correct sides) + spread + square-root impact + liquidity caps + partial fills. *Gate: strategy still has positive expectancy net of costs.*
4. **ROBUST VALIDATION.** Run CPCV (PBO, DSR), anchored + rolling walk-forward (decay ratio), bootstrap CIs, and White's RC / Hansen's SPA across the strategy family. Honestly count trials. *Gate: positive net OOS, DSR>0 with low PBO, survives SPA.* **Expect most current components (especially LSTM/Transformer and unvalidated SMC) to fail here — cut them.**
5. **PAPER / FORWARD TEST.** Run live paper trading via Upstox sandbox/websocket for a sustained period (months, enough trades) to confirm live execution matches backtest assumptions (slippage, fills, latency, token/disconnect handling). *Gate: live paper P&L tracks backtest within tolerance.*
6. **SMALL-CAPITAL LIVE, TIGHT LIMITS.** Deploy minimal capital with hard daily-loss and concentration limits, kill-switch, reconciliation, and human approval (stay under the algo OPS threshold; confirm SEBI/Upstox compliance). *Gate: weeks of stable, reconciled live operation with no control failures.*
7. **SCALE GRADUALLY** only as realized performance and risk controls prove out, re-checking market-impact assumptions as size grows.
